Have another question? Say hello ↗
What is Hubpas?+

Hubpas is an event discovery, event management and community app operated by Hubpas Limited. It helps you discover experiences, organise events, manage access and stay connected.

Who is Hubpas for?+

People who want to find events and the organisers who bring them to life. Hubpas is built around Sierra Leone’s event community. You must be at least 13 to use it.

Is creating an account free?+

Yes. You can create a free account. Eligible paid account options offer additional tools. Check the app for the features and conditions attached to each option.

What can organisers do?+

Create public or invitation-based events, add event details and media, manage RSVPs and ticket tiers, scan QR tickets, and explore supported donation, vendor and sponsorship tools. Features depend on the release and account.

How do tickets work?+

Your ticket records are available in the wallet area. Open a ticket to view its details and its code or QR code where applicable. Follow the organiser’s event and entry instructions. The Google Play release supports free-entry raffles only.

How do privacy and location permissions work?+

You choose whether to allow device permissions for features such as location sharing, maps, calls and uploads. Some features will not work without their related permission. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details and your choices.

Where can I get help?+

Email hubpasofficial@gmail.com. Never send your password, wallet PIN or verification codes. Some features depend on your device, account and release version.
