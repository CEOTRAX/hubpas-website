Have another question? Say hello ↗
What can I do on Hubpas?+

Discover events, connect with people and organisers, find tickets, explore venues and services, and manage your wallet. Features can vary by account and app version.

Is Hubpas just for events?+

Events are part of it. Hubpas also brings together communities, conversations, spaces, services, bookings and wallet tools.

Can I use Hubpas as an organiser?+

Yes. Open Hubpas to explore creating and managing events, offering spaces or services, and connecting with your audience. Availability depends on your account.

Where can I read about my privacy?+

Our Privacy Policy explains what information Hubpas collects, how it is used, your choices, and how to request account deletion.
