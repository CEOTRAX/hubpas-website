Have another question? Say hello ↗
What is Hubpas?+

Hubpas connects you with the places, people and experiences you care about. Discover gatherings, buy tickets with your wallet, organise your own and stay connected. Operated by Hubpas Limited.

Who is Hubpas for?+

People finding their next experience and organisers bringing people together: social gatherings, conferences, faith programmes, sports, learning and community occasions. You must be at least 13 to use Hubpas.

Is creating an account free?+

Yes. You can create a free account. Eligible paid account options offer additional tools. Check the app for the features and conditions attached to each option.

What can organisers do?+

Create public or invitation-based gatherings, set ticket tiers and share your link wherever your audience is. Eligible completed ticket purchases credit your Hubpas wallet instantly, after applicable fees. Payment processing and any holds can affect availability. Manage RSVPs, QR ticket scanning and supported donation, vendor and sponsorship tools. Features vary by release and account.

How do tickets work?+

Buy tickets using your Hubpas wallet and keep them together in the wallet area. Open a ticket to show its code or QR code where applicable. Follow the organiser’s entry instructions. The Google Play release supports free-entry raffles only.

How do privacy and location permissions work?+

You choose whether to allow device permissions for features such as location sharing, maps, calls and uploads. Some features will not work without their related permission. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details and your choices.

Where can I get help?+

Email hubpasofficial@gmail.com. Never send your password, wallet PIN or verification codes. Some features depend on your device, account and release version.
